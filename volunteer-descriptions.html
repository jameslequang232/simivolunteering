Disclaimer:

For most of these volunteer opportunities I do not list out all of the duties and or qualifications, just the main ones. Before volunteering, always check the location's official website. Hope you find a good place to volunteer! :)

Sewa

Duties: Volunteer's jobs will vary however just try to help out wherever is needed. Sewa is open Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday from 4:30 - 6:30, however volunteers are expected to arrive 15 minutes beforehand to help set up.

Qualifications/Prerequisites: N/A

Link to Website: https://www.sewausa.org/ .

Important: Sewa will be starting back up at a new location in January of 2025.

Youth Council

The Simi Valley Youth Council is an advisory board to the City Council on issues affecting youth in the community and is composed of 21 members and up to three alternates. Each high school and middle school is represented on the Youth Council, as well as the Community at Large.

Qualifications/Prerequisites: Must be in 7th-12th grade in the next school year.

Link to Website: https://www.simivalley.org/departments/city-manager-s-office/community-programs-and-facilities/youth-council .

There is an application process to get onto the Youth Council.
Locations for meetings will vary. To find location check out the Youth Council's website!

Simi Vallley Historical Society

Duties: "Provide tours on the history of Simi valley and help preserve items that hold historic value at Strathern Historical Park or help in gift shop. Hours= Monday-Friday: 9:00 A.M. to 3:00 P.M. Saturday-Sunday: 1:P.M. to 4:00 P.M."

Link to Website: https://www.simihistory.com/ .

Qualifications/Prerequisites: Junior Docent Program: Must be at least 12 years old and interested in the history of Simi Valley.

Rancho Simi Recreation and Prak District

Duties: Assist in special events, office help, and recreation classes. Also help may be needed with hiking trails and or preschool/afternoon programs.

Link to Website: https://www.rsrpd.org/ .

Qualifications/Prerequisites: Must be at least 12 years old and fill out an orientation on the website.

Volunteering locations will vary depending on the task given

Simi Valley Police Explorers

Duties: Explorers are trained in many areas of the law and assist the police during community events. Also explorers get to learn more on evidence collection and report writing.

Link to Website: https://www.svpdexplorers.com/

Qualifications/Prerequisites: Must attend weekly meetings held every Monday at 5:00 PM and be at least 14 years old or at least in 7th grade. Must also apply to get in.

Simi Valley Youth Orchestra

"At SVYO, we’re all about helping young musicians grow—both as artists and as people. We’re here to inspire a love of music, build lasting skills, and create a space where discipline meets the pure joy of making music. Do you have what it takes to be the elite of Simi Valley's young musicians? Then take this moment to sign up for an audition. We are excited to see what your musicianship brings to the orchestra as we take you on an incredible journey of musical discovery, growth and excellence.""

Link to Website: https://www.svyo.net/

Qualifications/Prerequisites: 19 years or younger and must audition.

Auditions for SVYO for 2025 have already elapsed, so you must wait until next year! Locations for events will vary.
